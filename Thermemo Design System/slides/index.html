04 — brand personality

how the brand feels.

moodquiet, warm, intimate
visualmonochrome, paper texture, cedar brown accent
voicesingkat, reflektif, casual-sentimental
energylow-key, tidak ramai
cultural cuejapanese-inspired, wabi-sabi, stamp/seal

thermemo is

  • a keepsake, not a content piece
  • slow even when the print is fast
  • for one person at a time
  • physical, on purpose

thermemo is not

  • another booth with stickers and props
  • loud, hype, or trendy
  • about influencer "moments"
  • a digital download

11 — photo direction

monochrome, only.

01 · pure
monochrome
hitam-putih bersih. default booth output.
02 · warm
warm monochrome
sepia lembut. nostalgic keepsake feel.
03 · sharp
high contrast
crushed blacks, paper highs. editorial.

do

  • tight crops on faces and hands
  • natural grain, slight underexposure
  • portrait orientation, 2:5 strip ratio

don't

  • filter neon, vivid color grading
  • stickers, cute overlays, kawaii props
  • busy backgrounds without clear subject

14 — do & don't

the short list.

✓do

  • whitespace luas — minimum 10% margin
  • foto monochrome, vertical 2:5 strip
  • cedar brown sebagai aksen kecil
  • copy pendek & meaningful
  • 記ノ片 konsisten — corner watermark

✗don't

  • ornamen jepang berlebihan (sakura, torii)
  • warna pastel ramai atau neon
  • terlalu banyak font dalam satu layout
  • layout penuh, padat, sesak
  • ubah icon jadi karakter lucu / emoji
